# Monitoring & Observability Engineering Guide

**A comprehensive knowledge base covering Monitoring and Observability concepts, tools, architectures, implementation, and practical engineering approaches.**

Welcome! 👋

Observability is more than collecting metrics, building dashboards, or configuring alerts. It's about understanding how systems behave, identifying why problems occur, and gaining the visibility needed to maintain reliable and performant applications and infrastructure.

This repository is an evolving collection of technical documentation, practical examples, hands-on labs, troubleshooting approaches, and engineering learnings covering the broader world of Monitoring and Observability.

The goal is to build a useful resource that takes you from fundamental concepts to advanced observability practices, with a strong focus on understanding, implementation, and practical application.

Whether you're starting your observability journey or working towards becoming a stronger SRE, DevOps, or Observability Engineer, this repository is designed to help you explore and understand the subject in depth.

---

## 🎯 What This Repository Covers

The aim is to explore the complete Observability ecosystem, including but not limited to:

- **Monitoring Fundamentals:** Monitoring strategies, monitoring architecture, monitoring vs observability, and monitoring methodologies.
- **Observability Fundamentals:** Metrics, logs, traces, telemetry, context propagation, and observability pillars.
- **Metrics & Instrumentation:** Metric types, application instrumentation, exporters, client libraries, and metric collection.
- **Prometheus:** Architecture, components, service discovery, scraping, PromQL, recording rules, and metric management.
- **Grafana:** Dashboards, visualization, data sources, dashboard design, and monitoring workflows.
- **Logging & Log Management:** Structured logging, log collection, processing, enrichment, storage, and analysis.
- **Distributed Tracing:** Traces, spans, trace context, sampling, distributed request flows, and trace analysis.
- **OpenTelemetry:** Architecture, SDKs, collectors, instrumentation, receivers, processors, exporters, and pipelines.
- **Alerting & Incident Detection:** Alert rules, alert design, notification management, alert fatigue, and incident response.
- **SRE & Reliability Engineering:** SLIs, SLOs, SLAs, error budgets, availability, latency, and reliability measurement.
- **Infrastructure & Application Monitoring:** Servers, containers, Kubernetes, cloud infrastructure, APIs, databases, and dependencies.
- **Performance & Capacity Engineering:** Performance analysis, resource utilization, saturation, bottleneck identification, and capacity planning.
- **Observability Architectures:** Data pipelines, collection strategies, storage, scalability, retention, and system design.
- **Troubleshooting & Debugging:** Root cause investigation, correlation of telemetry, incident analysis, and performance troubleshooting.
- **Advanced Observability Practices:** Cardinality management, sampling strategies, telemetry costs, instrumentation standards, and observability at scale.

The scope will continue to expand as new technologies, approaches, and engineering practices emerge.

## 💡 Engineering Philosophy

The focus is not simply on learning tools or collecting telemetry.

It's about understanding:

- What should we monitor and why?
- How do we know when an application is unhealthy?
- How can we identify the source of performance problems?
- How do metrics, logs, and traces work together?
- How should telemetry be collected, processed, and stored?
- How do we design meaningful alerts?
- How can observability improve troubleshooting and reduce MTTR?
- How do we build scalable and cost-effective observability systems?

**The objective is to move from collecting data to gaining actionable insights into system behaviour.**
